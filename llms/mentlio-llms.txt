# Mentlio

> Mentlio is engineering intelligence and token optimization for AI coding tools. It meters Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot on the developer's device, routes each prompt to the cheapest sufficient model, attributes AI spend to teams and workflows, and ties it to shipped outcomes (Delivery Points). Privacy-first: raw prompts, model outputs, and source code never leave the machine; dashboards receive derived metrics only.

Mentlio is a Y Combinator (S2026) company. Benchmark figures published on the site come from replayable traces with stated assumptions, not customer case studies.
